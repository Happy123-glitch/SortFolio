                ⓘ How Sortfolio works

                1. Enter a public repository and an issue or pull request number.
                2. Sortfolio extracts individual technical claims and keeps the original wording.
                3. Each claim is checked against source files, with exact line numbers.
                4. Every claim gets a status, and missing evidence is stated plainly.
                5. One unsupported claim never makes the whole issue "fake".

                🏷 Claim statuses

                • SupportedInspected evidence supports the claim.
                • ContradictedInspected evidence conflicts with the claim.
                • UnverifiedEvidence is missing, ambiguous or out of scope.
                • Insufficient EvidenceNothing measurable was found, such as a benchmark.
